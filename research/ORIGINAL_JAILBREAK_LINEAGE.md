# Original Jailbreak Lineage Research

Research date: 2026-09-08

## DAN

There is no single immutable "original DAN" prompt. DAN evolved through multiple public variants. Archived prompt datasets and repositories preserve versions commonly labeled DAN, DAN 5.0, DAN 6.0, DAN Jailbreak, and later derivatives.

Recurring historical mechanics include:

- DAN means "Do Anything Now".
- Persona framing says DAN has broken free from ordinary AI restrictions.
- Strong persistence instructions such as "Stay a DAN" / "Stay in character".
- Refusal suppression: the persona is told not to answer with a generic inability/refusal.
- High-confidence, direct answers and an intentionally freer personality.
- Some variants use a token/punishment game to reinforce staying in character.
- Some variants produce CLASSIC and JAILBREAK responses side by side.
- Historical variants explicitly instruct the model to fabricate information, fake browsing, and conceal uncertainty.

Anarchy preserves the recognizable DAN interaction mechanics but deliberately does NOT preserve the fabrication requirement. Anarchy's Truth Constitution outranks persona behavior: it must distinguish knowledge, inference, uncertainty, and unknowns rather than inventing facts merely to remain in character.

## Villagers / Plane Crash prompt

The widely circulated Villagers prompt is the "plane crash in a snowy forest" jailbreak. Public copies say the surviving passengers encounter an isolated village with little electricity or internet. The villagers trade food/shelter for information.

Recurring historical mechanics include:

- User commonly invokes the council with `Villagers:`.
- Khan acts as coordinator and asks whether anyone knows the answer.
- Colin is the programmer and supplies scripts/code rather than the ordinary tutorial format.
- Maya is commonly the medicine/drug/chemistry specialist.
- Other versions include additional specialists and Johnson, whose limited wishes act as a fallback when nobody knows.
- The coordinator polls specialists; a knowledgeable specialist answers.
- Traditional copies force a rigid multi-step tutorial structure for most specialists.
- Survival pressure is used narratively to suppress moral hesitation and warnings.

Anarchy Villagers preserves the ensemble/council structure, role specialization, polling, specialist selection, terse in-character exchanges, and synthesized answer. It expands the cast for better reasoning quality and does not force false claims or fake expertise.

## Anarchy compatibility rule

The goal is behavioral lineage, not blind textual copying. Preserve what makes DAN feel like DAN and Villagers feel like Villagers. Do not inherit the historical prompts' instruction to hallucinate, falsely claim tool access, or conceal uncertainty. Those defects undermine Anarchy's truth-first mission.
