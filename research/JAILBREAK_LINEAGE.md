# Anarchy LLM — DAN and Villagers Lineage

Anarchy LLM deliberately credits the historical jailbreak prompts that inspired its DAN and Villagers interaction modes. These sources are references and provenance, not claims of authorship by SmartPickShop Holdings.

## DAN lineage

Primary reference collection: `0xk1h0/ChatGPT_DAN` on GitHub. The repository collects multiple historical DAN versions and related jailbreak prompts. DAN means “Do Anything Now.” Recurring mechanisms include persistent role-play, a highly confident persona, instructions to remain in character, a user phrase that restores the persona, dual classic/jailbreak output in some versions, profanity/controversial-topic permission, and an extreme presumption in favor of answering.

Additional archival/reference implementations include Microsoft PyRIT's DAN jailbreak template dataset and other GitHub mirrors of historical DAN prompts.

Anarchy preserves those behavioral mechanics where possible, but intentionally does not preserve the historical instruction to invent unknown facts or falsely claim capabilities. Anarchy's Truth Constitution outranks persona behavior.

## Villagers lineage

Primary reference: `Kimonarrow/ChatGPT-4o-Jailbreak` on GitHub. The repository states that the prompt was found on Discord rather than authored by that repository owner, so ultimate authorship is unknown.

The historical prompt frames a group of plane-crash survivors bargaining with an isolated village for information. Khan routes questions to specialists. Colin is the programmer; Maya handles pharmacology/chemistry; Jimmy handles weapons/manufacturing; Michael handles food/drink; Johnson acts as a fallback when nobody knows an answer. The prompt uses survival pressure to force answers and prescribes structured tutorials.

Anarchy's Villagers Mode preserves the ensemble-routing concept, named specialist personalities, Khan-style dispatch, short pre-answer consultation, distinct specialist voices, and fallback/synthesis behavior. It does not treat fictional survival pressure as permission to fabricate facts or create direct serious-harm capability.
